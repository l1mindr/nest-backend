PORTFOLIO REALIZED P&L REPORT — MILESTONE M6.2
=============================================

1. OBJECTIVE
============

Extend the portfolio calculation domain (M6.1) with realized P&L and the
cost-basis policies that produce it:

  - SELL and TRANSFER_OUT now release cost basis instead of reducing quantity
    only.
  - Every SELL produces a realized P&L event (proceeds, released basis, signed
    gain/loss).
  - Three cost-basis strategies are implemented behind the existing
    CostBasisCalculator interface: AVERAGE, FIFO and LIFO.
  - The engine accepts a strategy by enum or an injected calculator instance.

As in M6.1, everything lives in the pure domain layer:

  src/features/portfolio/domain/calculation/

No migrations, no controllers, no DTOs, no Swagger, no application-layer
wiring, and no holdings mutation. Results are computed on demand and never
persisted.

2. SCOPE DECISIONS
==================

Scope confirmed before implementation:
  - Strategies: AVERAGE + FIFO + LIFO, selectable through the engine.
  - Surface: domain + engine only; no HTTP endpoint.

Design decisions (documented here so they are explicit, not accidental):

  - TRANSFER_OUT releases cost basis like a SELL but produces NO realized P&L
    event. A custody move out of the portfolio is not a disposal; it removes
    units (and their basis) from the position without realizing a gain. This
    keeps the average cost of the remainder unchanged and lets FIFO/LIFO
    consume the correct lots. (The M6.1 report deferred this exact decision;
    the price being optional on transfers makes "realized P&L on transfer-out"
    ill-defined, since proceeds cannot be computed without a sale price.)
  - P&L is reported GROSS of fees. `proceeds` is `amount × price`, the
    transaction fee is carried on the event (`fee`), and the application layer
    decides how to net it. Fees are still never merged into `totalCost`.
  - The opening position is modeled as a single lot for FIFO/LIFO whose
    per-unit cost is `openingCost / openingQuantity` — the only division in
    lot accounting; BUY lots carry their exact price. When the opening
    quantity is zero no opening lot exists (an orphaned opening cost is simply
    not attached to any lot).
  - TRANSFER_IN creates a zero-cost lot: it adds units without creating basis
    (a transfer price is preserved but never interpreted as acquisition cost),
    and the units must still be tracked so they can be sold or transferred out
    under FIFO/LIFO.
  - The engine still returns `averageCost`, now computed as the average of the
    remaining basis. With basis release this is invariant across sales for the
    AVERAGE policy.

4. STRATEGY SELECTION
=====================

  PortfolioCalculationEngine(costBasis: CostBasisCalculator | CostBasisStrategy)

  - Default: CostBasisStrategy.AVERAGE (backward compatible — the old no-arg
    and injected-calculator constructions behave exactly as before).
  - Enum values AVERAGE | FIFO | LIFO map to module-level stateless calculator
    singletons; an injected calculator instance is used directly.
  - The same ledger discriminates the policies:
      buy 1 @ 100, buy 1 @ 200, sell 1 @ 150 →
        AVERAGE: released 150, gain 0,   remaining cost 150
        FIFO:    released 100, gain 50,  remaining cost 200
        LIFO:    released 200, gain -50, remaining cost 100

5. REALIZED P&L EVENT
=====================

  { transactionId?, occurredAt, type: SELL, amount, proceeds,
    costBasisReleased, realizedGain, fee? }

  - proceeds = amount × price (exact).
  - costBasisReleased is the acquisition cost removed from the position:
    proportional share for AVERAGE, consumed lots for FIFO/LIFO.
  - realizedGain = proceeds − costBasisReleased (signed decimal string).
  - Events appear in chronological processing order; empty when no SELL.

6. AVERAGE-COST POLICY (M6.2)
=============================

  SELL:         releasedBasis = amount × (totalCost / quantityBefore)
                totalCost -= releasedBasis; event recorded.
  TRANSFER_OUT: same release, no event.
  TRANSFER_IN:  quantity += amount; no basis created.

  The average cost of the remainder is unchanged by a sale (mathematically
  exact except when the per-sale average is a non-terminating quotient, which
  is truncated at CALCULATION_DIVISION_MAX_FRACTION_DIGITS and documented in
  the code, mirroring M6.1's averageCost behavior).

  Example: buy 1 @ 50000, buy 1 @ 70000, sell 1 @ 90000 →
    released 60000, gain 30000, remainder { 1, 60000 } (avg unchanged at 60000).

7. FIFO / LIFO POLICIES
=======================

  Both are lot-based and share LotCostBasisCalculator; they differ only in the
  queue end a disposal consumes from (front = FIFO, back = LIFO).

  - BUY pushes a lot { amount, price } — per-unit cost is the exact BUY price.
  - Opening position is a single lot (see section 2).
  - TRANSFER_IN pushes a zero-cost lot.
  - SELL consumes lots in order and releases the exact sum of
    consumed × unitCost; proceeds/event computed as in section 5.
  - TRANSFER_OUT consumes lots identically but records no event.
  - Exhausted lots are dropped; the remaining lots keep acquisition order in
    the result regardless of which end was consumed (consumeLots builds the
    remainder by index in original order).

  consumeLots(lots, amount, fromBack) is the shared pure helper in lot.ts:
  it never mutates the input array, returns a fresh lot list and the released
  basis, and is unit-tested independently.

8. ERROR / INVARIANT HANDLING
=============================

  Reuses the M6.1 validation vocabulary unchanged. No new error codes were
  needed:
  - All three strategies share the extracted cost-basis-validation module.
  - Disposals exceeding the held quantity still fail deterministically with
    INSUFFICIENT_QUANTITY before any basis is released (the availability
    check always precedes the release computation, so division by a zero
    quantity cannot occur).
  - Malformed/negative amounts, prices and fees, unsupported types, and
    invalid opening state behave exactly as in M6.1.

9. PRECISION BEHAVIOR
=====================

  All arithmetic remains BigInt-based and exact where a finite decimal exists.
  Two unavoidable truncation points are documented and tested:

  - Average-cost per-sale release when totalCost / quantityBefore does not
    terminate: buy 1 @ 1, buy 2 @ 0.5, sell 1 @ 1 →
      released 0.66666666666666666666666666, gain 0.33333333333333333333333334
      (truncated toward zero at 26 fractional digits).
  - Opening-lot per-unit cost for FIFO/LIFO, same cap.

  BUY lots are always exact (released basis = sold × exact price), so a ledger
  with no opening position has zero approximation error under FIFO/LIFO.

12. EXPLICITLY DEFERRED WORK (M6.3+)
====================================

  - Unrealized P&L and current value (M6.3): the engine input gains a
    `currentPrice` supplied by the application layer; currentValue =
    quantity × currentPrice, unrealized = currentValue − remaining basis.
  - Fee accounting: realized P&L is gross of fees; the exact netting policy
    (buy fees into basis, sell fees against proceeds) is an application-layer
    decision and is deferred.
  - API surface: no GET /v1/portfolios/:portfolioId/pnl; CalculationError
    codes are still not mapped to HTTP errors.
  - Persistence: nothing is stored; no cached P&L columns.
  - Application-layer wiring: registering the engine (and a chosen default
    strategy) through Nest DI, and mapping domain inputs/outputs to repository
    data.

Concerns / design decisions
===========================

  - The AVERAGE strategy's disposal release reuses the same
    CALCULATION_DIVISION_MAX_FRACTION_DIGITS cap as averageCost, so per-sale
    basis and the reported average are consistent by construction. If the
    product ever needs a bounded presentation scale, that remains a
    presentation decision.
  - TRANSFER_OUT releasing basis is a behavior change from M6.1 (which kept
    totalCost untouched) and is intentional: the M6.1 report marked the
    transfer-out policy as deferred, and releasing basis on a custody move is
    required to keep the remainder's basis correct. The M6.1 tests that
    asserted the old behavior were updated as part of the feature commit.
  - The M6.2 report supersedes the "SELL keeps totalCost" note in the M6.1
    report's sections 9 and 20; SELL now releases basis under all three
    policies.
